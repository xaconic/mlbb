How ranking works

Rank order follows the system's Warrior-to-Mythical Immortal progression, then current stars, recorded win rate, and IGN. Win-rate sorting uses recorded win rate, then rank and stars. Match counts are not stored, so win rates are not weighted by matches. Ranks and statistics are entered by players and are not game-verified.
